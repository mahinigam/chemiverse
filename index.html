ENGINE ARCHITECTURE

How the instrument computes

No reaction lookup table. Products are generated from element property tensors, selected by constrained Gibbs minimisation, balanced exactly, and rendered from the resulting topology.

A

Element tensors

118 elements with electron configuration, Slater effective nuclear charge, Allen and Pauling electronegativity, ionization energies, electron affinities, radii, oxidation states, and phase data.

E

Quantum exceptions

Relativistic contraction, inert-pair stabilisation, ionic potential, polarising power, and optical absorption edges are derived from the electronic structure rather than stored as rules.

C

Bonding continuum

Van Arkel–Ketelaar ionic/covalent/metallic fractions, Hume-Rothery alloy rules, and kinetic barriers for noble-gas compounds.

B

Gibbs minimisation

A dense simplex solves the constrained linear program; continued-fraction rationalisation produces the smallest integer coefficients for the balanced equation.

D

Geometry

Thomson-point VSEPR domains on a sphere, lone-pair compression, and crystal-lattice generators for FCC, BCC, HCP, diamond, rock-salt, CsCl, fluorite, and zinc-blende.

Δ

Honest provenance

Published formation data is used where available; every fallback is marked estimated. Kinetic exclusions are reported, not hidden.
